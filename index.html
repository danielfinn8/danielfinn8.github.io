I study finance and AI, computing and data science at NYU Stern. I spent this past summer as a private equity analyst running diligence on a live acquisition. I grew up between the Chicago suburbs and Bengaluru, India.

Where I start

What I got from my grandfather

My mother's father, my grandpa, came to the United States from Guanajuato, Mexico when he was eighteen. Years later, he was the one who took care of me while both my parents were at work. I spent nearly every day with him, with his music always on.

The pride I have in being Mexican-American comes from him. He is the person I measure myself against, and most of what I believe about work I learned from watching him.

Five years

Growing up in Bengaluru

I lived in Bengaluru for five years. It's one of India's business and technology capitals, and that is something you notice living there: the traffic, the construction, and how much of the city is built around technology.

I went to Stonehill International School. The student body was about half international and half Indian, so I spent those five years around people from all over.

My first real exposure to business came from the back seat of a car. My dad works in finance, the drive to soccer practice was long, and he spent most of it on calls. I listened, then spent the rest of the drive asking him questions about what I'd heard. That was my spark.

Since

Back to Illinois, then New York

I moved back to Vernon Hills and started looking for a way into the business world I'd been hearing about. That turned into the Junior Economic Club and a philanthropic youth board, both on the Experience tab.

I'm now a sophomore at Stern studying finance alongside AI, computing and data science. Part of why I picked NYU is that it kept me in the kind of diverse environment I grew up in.

The work

What I've pitched

Here's the argument in each.

CopperOct 2025 · Capital Markets Club The copper market at $5.01/lb. A commodity pitch rather than a company one: market structure, the supply side, and the cases that follow from both. The first thing I presented at NYU.
VVVShort · Violet Quaker Valvoline. The Breeze Autocare acquisition needs synergies the business hasn't shown it can deliver, and leaves a debt burden either way. Consensus assumes locked-in cost headwinds get offset by productivity gains that haven't shown up.
DRILong · Finance Society Darden Restaurants. A consolidator priced as a secular decliner. Discounted growth, and scale economics that improve as consumer pressure pushes weaker operators out.
MDLZ / BRBRM&A · Finance Society Mondelēz acquires BellRing Brands. A hypothetical deal built on where protein and supplements are heading, with a three-statement model and segmented revenue builds underneath it.
BOOTLong · Violet Quaker Boot Barn. Tariffs read as a cost problem. For the biggest player in a fragmented market, they speed up consolidation instead.
